Dear Santa,

My name is Melvin and I live in Yellowknife, Northwest Territories. I hope this letter finds you well at the North Pole!

This year has been really special for me. I've been trying very hard to be good, and I have some wishes that I hope you can help with.

First, I would really love something that helps me be creative. Maybe something for drawing or painting? I'm not sure exactly what, but I love making art and my teacher says I'm getting better every day.

Second, I've been asking my parents for "something that goes really fast" - they're not sure what I mean, but I think you'll understand! It should be red if possible, because red is the fastest color.

Also, if it's not too much trouble, I'd like something educational. My mom always says learning is important, so maybe something about science or nature? But it should also be fun - I don't want boring homework stuff!

Oh, and one more thing - I really want something I can play with outside when the weather is nice. Something for sports or adventures, you know?

I know this is a lot to ask for, and I understand if you can't bring everything. Whatever you think is best would make me so happy!

Thank you for everything you do, Santa. You're the best!

Love,
Melvin

P.S. - I'll leave extra cookies this year! And carrots for the reindeer!
