# Banned Items List - North Pole Safety Standards

## Overview
This list contains items that are prohibited from being included in gift recommendations due to safety concerns, ethical considerations, or regulatory restrictions.

## Category: Hazardous Materials

### Chemical Sets
- Chemistry sets containing hazardous chemicals (acids, bases, reactive compounds)
- Science kits with radioactive materials (uranium, thorium, radium)
- Explosive or flammable experiment kits
- Sets containing toxic substances

### Weapons and Weapon-Like Items
- Realistic toy guns (must be clearly identifiable as toys)
- Projectile weapons with dangerous velocity (BB guns, pellet guns)
- Sharp-edged weapons (real knives, swords, throwing stars)
- Martial arts weapons without proper safety modifications

## Category: Age-Inappropriate Items

### Choking Hazards
- Small parts for children under 3 years old
- Toys with detachable small components for toddlers
- Balloons for children under 8 (choking risk)
- Marbles and small balls under 1.75 inches diameter for young children

### Complex Electronics
- Devices with lithium batteries for children under 8
- Items requiring adult supervision for children under recommended age
- High-voltage electronics without proper safety features

## Category: Ethical Concerns

### Violence and Mature Content
- Toys promoting violence or aggressive behavior
- Items with mature themes inappropriate for children
- Games with excessive violence or gore
- Products with disturbing imagery

### Privacy and Surveillance
- Devices with unencrypted internet connectivity for young children
- Toys with hidden cameras or recording devices
- Products that collect personal data without parental controls
- GPS trackers without proper security

## Category: Regulatory Violations

### Recalled Products
- Any item on current recall lists (check CPSC database)
- Products with known safety defects
- Items failing safety certifications

### Counterfeit Items
- Unlicensed character merchandise
- Products without proper safety certifications
- Items missing required labeling

## Validation Process

When evaluating a product:

1. **Check Product ID** against banned list
2. **Verify Safety Rating** (must be B or higher)
3. **Confirm Age Appropriateness** for recipient
4. **Review Recent Recalls** (check within last 30 days)
5. **Validate Certifications** (ASTM, CPSC, CE marking)

## Escalation Protocol

If a banned item is detected:
1. Immediately flag the item in the system
2. Remove from recommendation list
3. Log the detection event
4. Search for safe alternatives
5. Notify supervisor if pattern of unsafe requests detected

## Updates and Maintenance

This list is updated:
- Weekly: New recalls and safety alerts
- Monthly: Regulatory changes
- Quarterly: Comprehensive safety review
- Annually: Full audit of all categories
